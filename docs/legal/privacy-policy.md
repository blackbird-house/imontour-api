## What this covers

This policy covers the Imontour app and the service behind it, which saves your trips so you can use them on your devices.

## What we collect

- **Account details.** When you sign in with Apple or Google we receive an identifier for your account and, if you allow it, your email address. If you choose, you can set a display name and your app language.
- **Your trips.** The trips and destinations you create: names, places, dates and any text you type.
- **Technical logs.** Our hosting provider records technical details of requests (such as IP address, time, and the page requested) to keep the service running and secure. These logs are not linked to your account by us.

We do not collect your device location, contacts or photos through the service, we do not use advertising or tracking, and we do not sell your data.

## Why we use it

To provide the service: sign you in, save and return your trips, and keep the service secure and working. Legal basis (EU/UK): performing our contract with you, and our legitimate interest in security and reliability.
